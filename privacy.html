Effective Date: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile and desktop application Unstoppable Dursun (also known as Durdurulamaz Dursun / Stopman), developed and published by Mainstone Interactive.

We respect your privacy. Unstoppable Dursun does not require any account creation, user registration, or login credentials. We do not collect, store, or process sensitive personal data such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, government ID, or biometric data.

2. Local Network and Multiplayer Data

Our game includes same-device local multiplayer, online multiplayer, and Local Area Network (LAN) multiplayer functionality:

Unstoppable Dursun does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

5. Security

We value your trust. Because we do not operate remote user account databases or collect sensitive records, your personal data is not exposed to external database breaches through our application.
