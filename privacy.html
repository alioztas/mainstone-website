Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Unstoppable Dursun (also known as Durdurulamaz Dursun / Stopman), developed and published by Mainstone Interactive.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We respect your privacy. Unstoppable Dursun does not require any account creation, user registration, or login credentials. We do not collect, store, or process sensitive personal data such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or biometric data.

2. Local Network and Multiplayer Data

Our game includes same-device local multiplayer, online multiplayer, and Local Area Network (LAN) multiplayer functionality:

  • Local Network (LAN) & Multicast: The application uses local Wi-Fi UDP Multicast to discover other game sessions hosted on the same local subnet. These discovery packets and gameplay communication remain entirely within your private local network and are not transmitted to or stored on our external servers.
  • Online Multiplayer: Online peer connection data is handled strictly for active session synchronization and is never used to track your personal identity.

3. Advertising and Analytics (Third-Party Services)

The game may integrate trusted third-party services, such as Google AdMob, to serve advertisements. These providers may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic information and device identifiers (such as Apple IDFA or Google Advertising ID) in compliance with applicable platform privacy rules (such as Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework).

4. Children’s Privacy

Unstoppable Dursun does not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

5. Security

We value your trust. Since we do not collect or store personal user accounts or databases, your personal data is not exposed to external server breaches through our application.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or platform requirements. We advise you to review this page occasionally for any changes.
